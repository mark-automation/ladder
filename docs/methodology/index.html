How Ranking Works

Deterministic, versioned, auditable. Every rank references a model version; every change writes an immutable event with reasons. ML may assist later — it never becomes an opaque authority (blueprint §37).

Applicants — evidence first

Score = skills 35% · experience 25% · outcomes 20% · verifications 10% · leadership 10%. Then absolute evidence gates decide the tier; percentile caps bind only S/SS/SSS and only once a cohort has ≥ 500 assessed members.

Jobs — rubric, not curve

Authority + scope + complexity ± compensation-context modifier. Difficulty is a property of the work. SSS additionally requires top-of-population composite AND ≥50 qualified views — so grandiose postings can't self-promote into rarity.

Companies — behavior only

Payments, satisfaction, completion, disputes, clarity, responsiveness, fairness. No headcount, no brand. New companies cap at B until 20 completed contracts.
